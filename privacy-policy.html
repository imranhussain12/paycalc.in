Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

The short version

Everything you type into a calculator stays in your browser. We never receive, transmit or store your salary, pension or tax figures.

1. Calculator data

All calculations on PayCalc.in run client-side in JavaScript. Figures you enter are not sent to our servers, not stored in cookies, and disappear when you leave the page.

2. What we do and do not collect

We do not collect: your name, address, phone number, employee code, PAN, bank details, or any figure you enter into a calculator. There is no account system, no login, and no form on this site that submits your salary, pension or tax details anywhere.

What is collected automatically when you visit any website, including this one, is standard server and analytics data: your approximate location at country or state level, browser and device type, the pages you viewed, and the site that referred you. This is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

You can verify the first claim yourself. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and use any calculator on this site. You will see no request carrying your figures, because the arithmetic runs entirely in JavaScript in your browser.

3. Cookies in detail

We set no first-party cookies for the calculators themselves. Cookies you may encounter come from third parties:

  • Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Analytics providers may set cookies to distinguish repeat visits in aggregate.
  • Cloudflare, which serves this site, may set a cookie for security and bot-mitigation purposes.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and blocking them will not stop any calculator on this site from working.

4. Advertising and your choices

This site is supported by advertising, which is what keeps every calculator free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Your options:

  • Opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Opting out of personalised advertising does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant to you.

5. Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal information, we hold none to retain. Aggregate analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for understanding site traffic. Email correspondence is retained so that we can follow up on a query or correction, and is never sold or shared.

This site is served entirely over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access, correct or erase. If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence deleted, write to us and we will remove it. We honour browser "Do Not Track" signals to the extent our providers support them.

7. Links to other sites

We link frequently to government sources — the Department of Expenditure, DoPT, the Income Tax Department and others. Those sites have their own privacy practices, over which we have no control and for which we accept no responsibility. This policy applies only to paycalc.in.

8. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, approximate location (country/state), device type. This data is aggregated and does not identify you.

9. Advertising provider

We display advertising (Google AdSense) to keep the site free. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

10. Cookie summary

We set no first-party cookies for the calculators. Third-party cookies may be set by advertising and analytics providers as described above.

11. Email

If you email us, we keep the correspondence to respond and improve the site. We never sell or share your address.

12. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, with the date above revised accordingly.
