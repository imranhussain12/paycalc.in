Understanding a Central Government payslip

A typical payslip has three parts. Earnings: basic pay from your pay matrix cell, DA at the notified rate (60% from January 2026), HRA by city class, Transport Allowance with DA on it, and any department-specific allowances. Deductions: NPS at 10% of Basic + DA (the government's 14% goes straight to your PRAN and never appears as a deduction), CGHS by pay level, CGEGIS, and income-tax TDS. Net pay is what reaches your bank on the last working day of the month.

Every line on your payslip, explained

Basic pay is the cell you occupy in the pay matrix. Everything else is derived from it.

Dearness Allowance is 60% of basic from January 2026, revised each January and July. HRA is 27%, 18% or 9% of basic depending on whether your posting is in an X, Y or Z class city — nil if you occupy government quarters. Transport Allowance is a fixed monthly slab by pay level, and uniquely among allowances it earns DA on top: ₹3,600 becomes ₹5,760 at 60% DA.

On the deductions side, NPS takes 10% of Basic + DA from employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 — and the government separately credits 14% to your PRAN, which never appears as a deduction because it is not your money being taken. CGHS is a fixed monthly contribution by pay level: ₹250 for Levels 1–5, ₹450 for Level 6, ₹650 for Levels 7–11 and ₹1,000 from Level 12. CGEGIS costs ₹30, ₹60 or ₹120 a month by group and combines insurance with a small savings element. Finally income tax is deducted monthly by your DDO based on your annual declaration.

Why your actual payslip may differ

This calculator gives a close estimate, not a replica. Departments vary on several items: the CGHS slab in cities without a CGHS dispensary, licence fee where government accommodation is occupied, professional tax in states that levy it, society or cooperative recoveries, and departmental allowances such as risk, hardship, uniform, special duty or deputation allowance. Uniformed and field staff in particular draw allowances that no general calculator can anticipate.

Income tax here is spread evenly across twelve months, whereas a real DDO front-loads or back-loads depending on your declaration, so monthly deductions rarely match exactly even when the annual total does.

Where the leverage actually is

Three things move take-home more than anything else. City class: a transfer from Z to X roughly triples HRA on the same basic. Pay level: crossing into Level 6 changes both the transport allowance band and the CGHS slab. And tax regime choice: for employees below ₹12.75 lakh gross, the new regime removes income tax entirely — check yours in the income tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What deductions apply to a Central Government salary?

Typical deductions: NPS employee contribution (10% of Basic + DA), CGHS subscription (₹250–₹1,000 per month by pay level), CGEGIS insurance (₹30–₹120), income tax TDS, and optional recoveries such as licence fee for government quarters.

What is Transport Allowance (TPTA)?

A fixed monthly allowance: ₹3,600 + DA for Levels 3–8 (₹7,200 + DA for Level 9 and above) in higher-TPTA cities, and half those base amounts elsewhere. Levels 1–2 receive ₹1,350/₹900 + DA.

Does DA apply to allowances?

DA itself is a percentage of basic pay. Transport Allowance additionally earns DA on itself. HRA does not earn DA.

Why is my actual take-home slightly different?

Departments differ on CGHS slab, professional recoveries, quarters licence fees and TDS spread. This calculator gives a close estimate, not your exact payslip.

Why does the government's 14% NPS not appear as a deduction?

Because it is not deducted from you. Your 10% of Basic + DA is deducted; the government separately credits 14% into your PRAN as an employer contribution. Both appear in your NPS statement, but only yours reduces take-home pay.

What is CGEGIS and how much does it cost?

The Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme provides life cover with a small savings component. The monthly subscription is ₹120 for Group A, ₹60 for Group B and ₹30 for Group C.

Why is my actual take-home different from this estimate?

Departments differ on CGHS slab, quarters licence fee, professional tax where a state levies it, society recoveries and departmental allowances such as risk, hardship or deputation allowance. TDS is also spread differently by each DDO.
