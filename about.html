About PayCalc.in

PayCalc.in exists for one reason: government pay in India is governed by precise, published rules — yet finding a straight answer to "what will my salary be?" means wading through gazette PDFs, circulars and contradictory news reports.

We build free calculators and plain-English guides for Central Government employees, pensioners and their families. Teachers, railway staff, postal employees, CAPF personnel, clerks, officers — anyone whose pay is set by a Pay Commission.

Our principles

  • Sourced, not invented. Every rate traces to a Department of Expenditure order, gazette notification or official portal, linked where it is used.
  • Projections labelled as projections. The 8th CPC report is not out; anything based on it is clearly marked as a scenario, never presented as fact.
  • Private by design. Calculations run entirely in your browser. Your salary figures are never uploaded, stored or seen by us.
  • Free, always. The site is supported by advertising, not by selling tools or data.

Who runs this

PayCalc.in is built by the developer of Calculious.com (financial calculators for four countries) and RenterPlaybook.com (state-by-state tenant law guides) — sites with the same philosophy: official sources, clean tools, no nonsense.

Corrections

Spotted an outdated rate or an error? Please tell us — corrections ship fast.

Disclaimer: PayCalc.in is an independent educational website. It is not affiliated with the Government of India or any of its departments. Always verify figures with your Pay & Accounts Office.
