8th CPC Level 7 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 7 · GP 4600 · Group B

Level 7 covers Superintendents, Section Officers (entry), Assistant Section Officers, Inspectors (CBIC/CBDT). Current basic pay runs ₹44,900–₹1,42,400; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹1,15,393 and ₹1,34,700 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 7

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 7 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹9,61,758 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹56,574 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 7?

Level 7 (Grade Pay 4600) is a Group B level covering Assistant Section Officers in the Central Secretariat Service, Inspectors in CBIC and CBDT, Superintendents, Assistant Enforcement Officers, and Sub-Divisional Officers in engineering cadres.

How people get here: SSC CGL is the dominant route — Inspector (Income Tax / Central Excise) and Assistant Section Officer are among the most sought-after results of that examination.

Level 7 is where the CGHS contribution rises to ₹650 a month, and where most graduates entering through SSC CGL begin their careers.

Career progression from Level 7

Level 7 feeds into Level 8, and in the CSS to Section Officer at Level 8, then Under Secretary at Level 11.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 7 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 8 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹44,900, it takes roughly 39 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹1,42,400.

Pension outlook for Level 7

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 7:

  • At the entry stage of ₹44,900 → basic pension of about ₹22,450 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹1,42,400 → basic pension of about ₹71,200 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

An officer entering at Level 7 in their mid-twenties and progressing normally through Levels 8, 9 and 11 typically retires around Level 12, with a pension well above ₹1 lakh a month at current rates.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹22,450 would be revised to roughly ₹64,207. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 7?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 7 (GP 4600) runs from ₹44,900 at entry to ₹1,42,400 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 7 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹1,15,393 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹1,28,414 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹1,34,700 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 7 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 7 employee at entry draws ₹71,840 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹1,28,414 — a real gain of about ₹56,574 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 7 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 7 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹9,61,758 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 7?

Level 7 (Grade Pay 4600) covers Assistant Section Officers in the Central Secretariat Service, Inspectors in CBIC and CBDT, Superintendents, Assistant Enforcement Officers, and Sub-Divisional Officers in engineering cadres.

How do I get promoted from Level 7?

Level 7 feeds into Level 8, and in the CSS to Section Officer at Level 8, then Under Secretary at Level 11. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 8 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 7?

Level 7 falls in the Levels 3–8 transport allowance band: ₹3,600 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹1,800 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹650 per month and CGEGIS is ₹60 as a Group B employee.

What pension will a Level 7 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 7 entry stage of ₹44,900 gives about ₹22,450 per month, rising to about ₹71,200 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
