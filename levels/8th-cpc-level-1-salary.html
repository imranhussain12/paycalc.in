8th CPC Level 1 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 1 · GP 1800 · Group C

Level 1 covers Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS), peons, attendants, safai karamcharis. Current basic pay runs ₹18,000–₹56,900; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹46,260 and ₹54,000 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 1

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 1 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹3,85,560 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹22,680 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 1?

Level 1 (Grade Pay 1800) is a Group C level covering Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS), safaiwalas, chowkidars, daftaries, gardeners and peons across every central ministry, plus Group D equivalents in the Railways and India Post.

How people get here: SSC Multi-Tasking Staff examination, open to candidates who have passed Class 10. It is the single largest entry point into central government service.

Level 1 has the longest column in the matrix — 40 stages — so an MTS recruited at 20 can spend an entire career inside the level and still reach ₹56,900 basic through annual increments alone.

Career progression from Level 1

Promotion to Level 2 (Lower Division Clerk) comes through a departmental seniority quota or a limited departmental competitive examination, typically after 3–8 years depending on the ministry’s vacancy position.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 1 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 2 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹18,000, it takes roughly 39 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹56,900.

Pension outlook for Level 1

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 1:

  • At the entry stage of ₹18,000 → basic pension of about ₹9,000 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹56,900 → basic pension of about ₹28,450 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

Because pension is 50% of last basic drawn, an MTS retiring near the top of Level 1 secures a far better pension than one who stagnates mid-column, which is why MACP upgradations matter enormously at this level.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹9,000 would be revised to roughly ₹25,740. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 1?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 1 (GP 1800) runs from ₹18,000 at entry to ₹56,900 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 1 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹46,260 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹51,480 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹54,000 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 1 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 1 employee at entry draws ₹28,800 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹51,480 — a real gain of about ₹22,680 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 1 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 1 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹3,85,560 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 1?

Level 1 (Grade Pay 1800) covers Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS), safaiwalas, chowkidars, daftaries, gardeners and peons across every central ministry, plus Group D equivalents in the Railways and India Post.

How do I get promoted from Level 1?

Promotion to Level 2 (Lower Division Clerk) comes through a departmental seniority quota or a limited departmental competitive examination, typically after 3–8 years depending on the ministry’s vacancy position. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 2 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 1?

Level 1 falls in the Levels 1–2 transport allowance band: ₹1,350 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹900 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹250 per month and CGEGIS is ₹30 as a Group C employee.

What pension will a Level 1 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 1 entry stage of ₹18,000 gives about ₹9,000 per month, rising to about ₹28,450 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
