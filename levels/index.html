Not sure which level you are on?

Your pay level appears on your payslip, usually as "Level" or "Pay Level" next to your basic pay. If it is not shown, you can work it out from your pre-2016 grade pay:

What each level page covers

Every page gives you the same six things for that specific level: the posts and cadres it covers and how people are recruited into them; projected basic pay at fitment factors 2.28, 2.57 and 2.86; gross salary with HRA for X, Y and Z class cities; the transport allowance band, CGHS contribution and NPS figures that apply at that level; an arrears estimate; and the pension outlook including what an 8th CPC revision would mean.

Each page also links straight into the 8th CPC calculator pre-filled with that level's entry pay, so you can adjust to your own stage in the column.

A note on these figures

All pages use the entry stage of each level — the first cell in the column. If you have served several years at your level you will be further down it, and your basic pay will be higher, because each annual increment adds 3%. Use the calculator with your actual basic pay for a figure that matches your payslip.

And as everywhere on this site: the 8th CPC report has not been published, so every projected figure is a scenario rather than a promise.
