8th CPC Level 16 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 16 · HAG+ · Group A

Level 16 covers Special Secretaries, Directors General. Current basic pay runs ₹2,05,400–₹2,24,400; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹5,27,878 and ₹6,16,200 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 16

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 16 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹43,99,668 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹2,58,804 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 16?

Level 16 (HAG+) is a Group A level covering Special Secretaries, Directors General of major organisations, and Members of statutory boards.

How people get here: Promotion within the Higher Administrative Grade structure.

Level 16 spans barely ₹19,000 from entry to maximum, the narrowest band in the matrix apart from the fixed apex levels.

Career progression from Level 16

Level 17, the apex scale, follows for the small number of officers who reach Secretary rank.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 16 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 17 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹2,05,400, it takes roughly 3 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹2,24,400.

Pension outlook for Level 16

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 16:

  • At the entry stage of ₹2,05,400 → basic pension of about ₹1,02,700 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹2,24,400 → basic pension of about ₹1,12,200 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

Pension at this level is close to the structural maximum for central government service.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹1,02,700 would be revised to roughly ₹2,93,722. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 16?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 16 (HAG+) runs from ₹2,05,400 at entry to ₹2,24,400 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 16 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹5,27,878 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹5,87,444 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹6,16,200 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 16 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 16 employee at entry draws ₹3,28,640 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹5,87,444 — a real gain of about ₹2,58,804 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 16 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 16 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹43,99,668 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 16?

Level 16 (HAG+) covers Special Secretaries, Directors General of major organisations, and Members of statutory boards.

How do I get promoted from Level 16?

Level 17, the apex scale, follows for the small number of officers who reach Secretary rank. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 17 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 16?

Level 16 falls in the Level 9 and above transport allowance band: ₹7,200 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹3,600 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹1,000 per month and CGEGIS is ₹120 as a Group A employee.

What pension will a Level 16 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 16 entry stage of ₹2,05,400 gives about ₹1,02,700 per month, rising to about ₹1,12,200 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
