8th CPC Level 12 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 12 · GP 7600 · Group A

Level 12 covers Junior Administrative Grade — Deputy Secretaries. Current basic pay runs ₹78,800–₹2,09,200; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹2,02,516 and ₹2,36,400 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 12

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 12 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹16,87,896 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹99,288 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 12?

Level 12 (Grade Pay 7600) is a Group A level covering Junior Administrative Grade — Deputy Secretaries in central ministries, Joint Commissioners, and Superintendents of Police in larger districts.

How people get here: Promotion at roughly nine years of Group A service, subject to empanelment and vacancy.

From Level 12 the CGHS contribution rises to ₹1,000 a month and CGEGIS moves to the Group A rate.

Career progression from Level 12

Level 13, the Selection Grade or Director level, follows at about 13–14 years.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 12 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 13 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹78,800, it takes roughly 33 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹2,09,200.

Pension outlook for Level 12

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 12:

  • At the entry stage of ₹78,800 → basic pension of about ₹39,400 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹2,09,200 → basic pension of about ₹1,04,600 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

Level 12 is the point from which non-functional upgradation rules begin to matter for officers of organised Group A services relative to their IAS batchmates.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹39,400 would be revised to roughly ₹1,12,684. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 12?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 12 (GP 7600) runs from ₹78,800 at entry to ₹2,09,200 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 12 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹2,02,516 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹2,25,368 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹2,36,400 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 12 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 12 employee at entry draws ₹1,26,080 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹2,25,368 — a real gain of about ₹99,288 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 12 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 12 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹16,87,896 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 12?

Level 12 (Grade Pay 7600) covers Junior Administrative Grade — Deputy Secretaries in central ministries, Joint Commissioners, and Superintendents of Police in larger districts.

How do I get promoted from Level 12?

Level 13, the Selection Grade or Director level, follows at about 13–14 years. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 13 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 12?

Level 12 falls in the Level 9 and above transport allowance band: ₹7,200 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹3,600 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹1,000 per month and CGEGIS is ₹120 as a Group A employee.

What pension will a Level 12 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 12 entry stage of ₹78,800 gives about ₹39,400 per month, rising to about ₹1,04,600 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
