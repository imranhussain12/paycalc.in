8th CPC Level 4 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 4 · GP 2400 · Group C

Level 4 covers Upper Division Clerks, senior technicians, head constables. Current basic pay runs ₹25,500–₹81,100; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹65,535 and ₹76,500 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 4

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 4 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹5,46,210 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹32,130 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 4?

Level 4 (Grade Pay 2400) is a Group C level covering Upper Division Clerks, Stenographer Grade D, Head Constables in the CAPFs and Delhi Police, and Junior Clerks in the Railways.

How people get here: SSC CHSL for stenographer posts and SSC CGL for some UDC-equivalent posts, plus promotion from Levels 2 and 3.

Level 4 sits just below the Grade Pay 2800 ceiling, where a large number of clerical staff spend their middle career years.

Career progression from Level 4

Onward movement is to Level 5 and then Level 6, where the step from Grade Pay 2800 to 4200 is the single biggest percentage jump in the Group C ladder.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 4 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 5 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹25,500, it takes roughly 39 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹81,100.

Pension outlook for Level 4

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 4:

  • At the entry stage of ₹25,500 → basic pension of about ₹12,750 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹81,100 → basic pension of about ₹40,550 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

Reaching Level 6 before retirement roughly doubles the pension of an employee who retires from Level 4, which is why the Grade Pay 4200 barrier dominates cadre-restructuring demands.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹12,750 would be revised to roughly ₹36,465. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 4?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 4 (GP 2400) runs from ₹25,500 at entry to ₹81,100 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 4 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹65,535 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹72,930 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹76,500 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 4 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 4 employee at entry draws ₹40,800 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹72,930 — a real gain of about ₹32,130 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 4 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 4 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹5,46,210 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 4?

Level 4 (Grade Pay 2400) covers Upper Division Clerks, Stenographer Grade D, Head Constables in the CAPFs and Delhi Police, and Junior Clerks in the Railways.

How do I get promoted from Level 4?

Onward movement is to Level 5 and then Level 6, where the step from Grade Pay 2800 to 4200 is the single biggest percentage jump in the Group C ladder. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 5 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 4?

Level 4 falls in the Levels 3–8 transport allowance band: ₹3,600 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹1,800 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹250 per month and CGEGIS is ₹30 as a Group C employee.

What pension will a Level 4 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 4 entry stage of ₹25,500 gives about ₹12,750 per month, rising to about ₹40,550 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
