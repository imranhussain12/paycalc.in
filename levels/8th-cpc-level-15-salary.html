8th CPC Level 15 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 15 · HAG · Group A

Level 15 covers Additional Secretaries, Additional DGs. Current basic pay runs ₹1,82,200–₹2,24,100; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹4,68,254 and ₹5,46,600 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 15

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 15 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹39,02,724 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹2,29,572 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 15?

Level 15 (Higher Administrative Grade) is a Group A level covering Additional Secretaries to the Government of India, Additional Directors General of Police, and heads of major attached offices.

How people get here: Promotion at roughly 25–30 years of service, subject to empanelment.

The Higher Administrative Grade compresses sharply — the whole of Level 15 spans only ₹1,82,200 to ₹2,24,100.

Career progression from Level 15

Level 16 (HAG+) is next, followed by the apex scales.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 15 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 16 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹1,82,200, it takes roughly 7 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹2,24,100.

Pension outlook for Level 15

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 15:

  • At the entry stage of ₹1,82,200 → basic pension of about ₹91,100 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹2,24,100 → basic pension of about ₹1,12,050 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

From Level 15 upward pension approaches the ceiling, since apex pay itself is capped at ₹2,50,000.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹91,100 would be revised to roughly ₹2,60,546. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 15?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 15 (HAG) runs from ₹1,82,200 at entry to ₹2,24,100 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 15 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹4,68,254 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹5,21,092 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹5,46,600 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 15 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 15 employee at entry draws ₹2,91,520 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹5,21,092 — a real gain of about ₹2,29,572 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 15 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 15 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹39,02,724 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 15?

Level 15 (Higher Administrative Grade) covers Additional Secretaries to the Government of India, Additional Directors General of Police, and heads of major attached offices.

How do I get promoted from Level 15?

Level 16 (HAG+) is next, followed by the apex scales. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 16 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 15?

Level 15 falls in the Level 9 and above transport allowance band: ₹7,200 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹3,600 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹1,000 per month and CGEGIS is ₹120 as a Group A employee.

What pension will a Level 15 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 15 entry stage of ₹1,82,200 gives about ₹91,100 per month, rising to about ₹1,12,050 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
