8th CPC Level 8 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 8 · GP 4800 · Group B

Level 8 covers Senior Section Officers, Assistant Audit Officers, Income Tax Officers. Current basic pay runs ₹47,600–₹1,51,100; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹1,22,332 and ₹1,42,800 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 8

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 8 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹10,19,592 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹59,976 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 8?

Level 8 (Grade Pay 4800) is a Group B level covering Section Officers in the Central Secretariat, Assistant Audit Officers and Assistant Accounts Officers in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department, and Income Tax Officers.

How people get here: Mainly promotion from Level 7, with a limited departmental competitive examination route. Assistant Audit Officer is filled directly through SSC CGL.

Level 8 is the highest Group B gazetted grade in many organisations and the point at which officers begin exercising independent statutory powers.

Career progression from Level 8

Level 8 typically leads to Level 9, or directly to Level 10 in departments that operate a non-functional upgradation.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 8 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 9 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹47,600, it takes roughly 39 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹1,51,100.

Pension outlook for Level 8

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 8:

  • At the entry stage of ₹47,600 → basic pension of about ₹23,800 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹1,51,100 → basic pension of about ₹75,550 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

Assistant Audit Officers recruited directly at this level enjoy an unusually long runway, often retiring at Level 12 or above.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹23,800 would be revised to roughly ₹68,068. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 8?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 8 (GP 4800) runs from ₹47,600 at entry to ₹1,51,100 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 8 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹1,22,332 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹1,36,136 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹1,42,800 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 8 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 8 employee at entry draws ₹76,160 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹1,36,136 — a real gain of about ₹59,976 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 8 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 8 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹10,19,592 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 8?

Level 8 (Grade Pay 4800) covers Section Officers in the Central Secretariat, Assistant Audit Officers and Assistant Accounts Officers in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department, and Income Tax Officers.

How do I get promoted from Level 8?

Level 8 typically leads to Level 9, or directly to Level 10 in departments that operate a non-functional upgradation. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 9 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 8?

Level 8 falls in the Levels 3–8 transport allowance band: ₹3,600 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹1,800 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹650 per month and CGEGIS is ₹60 as a Group B employee.

What pension will a Level 8 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 8 entry stage of ₹47,600 gives about ₹23,800 per month, rising to about ₹75,550 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
