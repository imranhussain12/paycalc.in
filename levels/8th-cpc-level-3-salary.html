8th CPC Level 3 Salary: Projected Pay & Arrears

Updated August 2026 Pay Level 3 · GP 2000 · Group C

Level 3 covers Constables (CAPF/Delhi Police), skilled tradesmen, railway technicians. Current basic pay runs ₹21,700–₹69,100; under the 8th Pay Commission the entry basic is projected between ₹55,769 and ₹65,100 depending on the fitment factor.

Arrears estimate for Level 3

With the 8th CPC effective 1 January 2026 and an assumed implementation of May 2027 (17 months), a Level 3 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹4,64,814 at factor 2.86 (monthly difference ₹27,342 × 17). Model other dates and factors in the arrears calculator.

Who works at Level 3?

Level 3 (Grade Pay 2000) is a Group C level covering Constables in the CAPFs (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB) and Delhi Police, skilled tradesmen and technicians in the Railways and defence establishments.

How people get here: SSC GD Constable examination and force-specific recruitment drives, plus Railway RRB Group D and technician examinations.

Uniformed personnel at this level draw additional risk and hardship allowances that civilian staff do not, so the payslip often exceeds what the matrix alone suggests.

Career progression from Level 3

Constables move to Head Constable (Level 4) on seniority and departmental tests; technicians progress through the skilled and highly-skilled grades.

Employees who are not promoted still move up financially through the Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP) scheme, which grants three financial upgradations to the next level in the matrix after 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous service. A Level 3 employee who never receives a functional promotion would still expect to reach roughly Level 4 on their first MACP.

Within the level, pay rises by an annual increment of 3% of basic pay, rounded up to the next ₹100, granted on 1 January or 1 July depending on when you joined or were last promoted. From the entry stage of ₹21,700, it takes roughly 39 increments to reach the top of the column at ₹69,100.

Pension outlook for Level 3

Central government pension is 50% of the last basic pay drawn (or average emoluments of the final ten months, whichever is higher). Retiring from Level 3:

  • At the entry stage of ₹21,700 → basic pension of about ₹10,850 per month
  • At the top of the column, ₹69,100 → basic pension of about ₹34,550 per month
  • Dearness Relief is paid on top at the same percentage as DA, and family pension is normally 30% of last pay drawn

CAPF personnel recruited on or after 1 January 2004 are on NPS; the long-running demand for restoration of the old pension scheme for paramilitary forces is one of the live issues before the 8th CPC.

If the 8th CPC applies a fitment factor of 2.86 to pensions as well as pay — the pattern followed at every previous changeover — a pension of ₹10,850 would be revised to roughly ₹31,031. Model your own in the pension calculator.

Employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System rather than the old defined-benefit pension, and since April 2025 may instead opt for the Unified Pension Scheme, which assures 50% of the average basic pay of the final twelve months after 25 years of service.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current basic pay for Level 3?

Under the 7th CPC pay matrix, Level 3 (GP 2000) runs from ₹21,700 at entry to ₹69,100 at the top of the scale, with 3% annual increments.

What will Level 3 salary be under the 8th Pay Commission?

At entry, projected new basic pay is about ₹55,769 with fitment factor 2.57, ₹62,062 at 2.86 (the union demand), or ₹65,100 at 3.00. The official factor will be known only when the 8th CPC report is published.

How much will a Level 3 employee gain per month?

Today a Level 3 employee at entry draws ₹34,720 as Basic + DA (60%). At factor 2.86 the new basic would be ₹62,062 — a real gain of about ₹27,342 per month before allowances.

How much 8th CPC arrears would Level 3 get?

If implemented in May 2027 with effect from January 2026 (17 months), a Level 3 employee at entry pay would accrue roughly ₹4,64,814 in arrears at factor 2.86, before tax and adjustments.

Which posts come under Level 3?

Level 3 (Grade Pay 2000) covers Constables in the CAPFs (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB) and Delhi Police, skilled tradesmen and technicians in the Railways and defence establishments.

How do I get promoted from Level 3?

Constables move to Head Constable (Level 4) on seniority and departmental tests; technicians progress through the skilled and highly-skilled grades. If no promotion arises, the MACP scheme grants a financial upgradation to roughly Level 4 after 10 years of service, with two further upgradations at 20 and 30 years.

What is the transport allowance and CGHS deduction at Level 3?

Level 3 falls in the Levels 3–8 transport allowance band: ₹3,600 per month in higher-TPTA cities and ₹1,800 elsewhere, plus DA on that amount. The CGHS contribution is ₹250 per month and CGEGIS is ₹30 as a Group C employee.

What pension will a Level 3 employee get?

Pension is 50% of last basic pay. Retiring at the Level 3 entry stage of ₹21,700 gives about ₹10,850 per month, rising to about ₹34,550 at the top of the column, plus Dearness Relief. Employees who joined from 1 January 2004 are on NPS or UPS instead.
