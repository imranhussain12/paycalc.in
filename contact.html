What to write to us about

  • Corrections — an outdated DA rate, a wrong city classification, a calculation that does not match your payslip. Please include the page link and, if possible, a source.
  • Calculator requests — a tool you wish existed (e.g. MACP fixation, leave encashment, gratuity).
  • Guide suggestions — a rule you want explained in plain English.

What to expect

We aim to reply within two to three working days. Corrections are prioritised over everything else — if a rate on this site is wrong, employees may be making decisions on it, so those get fixed the same day wherever possible.

How to report an error usefully

The most helpful corrections include three things: the page link, the figure you believe is wrong along with what it should be, and a source if you have one — a Department of Expenditure office memorandum, a gazette notification, or your own payslip with personal details removed.

We are particularly glad to hear from employees whose payslip does not match our take-home calculator. Departments differ on CGHS slabs, licence fees, professional tax and departmental allowances, and the only way we learn about those variations is when someone tells us.

What we cannot do

We are an independent educational website, not a government office, and there are things we genuinely cannot help with:

  • Individual pay fixation disputes. Only your Pay & Accounts Office or Drawing and Disbursing Officer can examine your service record and fix your pay.
  • Pension sanction or PPO problems. These go to your Pension Disbursing Authority or the Department of Pension & Pensioners' Welfare.
  • Personalised tax or investment advice. We are not tax practitioners or financial advisers. For filing questions, consult a qualified professional or the Income Tax Department's helpline.
  • Predicting the 8th CPC outcome. We genuinely do not know what the fitment factor will be, and neither does anyone else outside the commission.
  • Recruitment, transfers or service matters. These rest with your department and the relevant recruiting agency.

Official sources

For authoritative answers, these are the right doors to knock on:

  • Department of Expenditure — pay, allowances and DA orders
  • Department of Personnel & Training — service rules, MACP and cadre matters
  • Department of Pension & Pensioners' Welfare — pension rules and PPO issues
  • Income Tax Department — filing, Form 10E and Section 89(1) relief
  • PFRDA — NPS and the Unified Pension Scheme

We aim to reply within 2–3 working days. We cannot provide personalised pay fixation, legal or tax advice — for official matters, contact your DDO, Pay & Accounts Office or a qualified professional.
