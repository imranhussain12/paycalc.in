HRA Rules for Central Government Employees

Updated August 2026 By the PayCalc.in team · Sources cited throughout

Which city class you are in decides your House Rent Allowance. The classification, the rates, the minimum floors and the DA-linked escalator — all in one place.

The three city classes

ClassCriterionHRA rateMinimum HRA
XPopulation 50 lakh+ (8 cities)27%₹5,400
YPopulation 5–50 lakh (~90 cities)18%₹3,600
ZAll other places9%₹1,800

X class cities: Delhi (NCR areas as notified), Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. Y class includes most state capitals and large cities — Lucknow, Jaipur, Patna, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Guwahati, Indore, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Kochi, Visakhapatnam and others. Everything else is Z. The list derives from census population and is notified by the Department of Expenditure (DoE orders).

The DA-linked escalator

The 7th CPC set HRA initially at 24/16/8 (down from the 6th CPC's 30/20/10, because the base pay had grown 2.57×) with automatic restoration steps:

  • DA crosses 25% → HRA becomes 27/18/9 (happened July 2021)
  • DA crosses 50% → HRA becomes 30/20/10 (DA reached 50% in January 2024)

Departments implemented the 30/20/10 restoration per DoE orders; some establishments continued 27/18/9 pending clarification, which is why our HRA calculator defaults to the conservative 27/18/9 — edit as your office applies.

Which cities fall in which class

Classification follows census population and is notified by the Department of Expenditure. It is revised after each census, so a city can move up a class and employees there begin drawing the higher rate from the notified date.

X class (population 50 lakh and above) — eight cities: Delhi (and the notified NCR areas), Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad.

Y class (5 to 50 lakh) — roughly ninety cities, including most state capitals and large district headquarters: Lucknow, Kanpur, Jaipur, Patna, Bhopal, Indore, Nagpur, Chandigarh, Guwahati, Bhubaneswar, Coimbatore, Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Madurai, Varanasi, Agra, Meerut, Ludhiana, Amritsar, Rajkot, Surat, Vadodara, Nashik, Aurangabad, Jodhpur, Ranchi, Raipur, Dehradun, Jammu, Srinagar and Mysuru, among others.

Z class — everywhere else. Most district and sub-divisional postings fall here, which is why field staff draw materially less HRA than colleagues doing identical work in a state capital.

Your entitlement follows your place of posting, not where your family lives or where you own property. An officer posted to a Z class town draws 9% even if the family remains in Delhi.

Situations that change your HRA

  • Government quarters. If you are allotted accommodation you draw no HRA and instead pay a licence fee. Employees who decline an allotment generally forfeit HRA for the period, subject to departmental rules.
  • Spouse also in government service. Only one of you may draw HRA if you share government accommodation. Both may draw HRA if living in private accommodation.
  • On tour or temporary duty. HRA continues at your headquarters rate; TA/DA rules cover the temporary station separately.
  • On leave. HRA generally continues during leave up to 180 days, subject to certification requirements for longer periods.
  • On deputation. Entitlement usually follows the borrowing organisation's station classification.
  • Minimum HRA. HRA cannot fall below ₹5,400 (X), ₹3,600 (Y) or ₹1,800 (Z) per month — floors derived from 30%, 20% and 10% of the ₹18,000 minimum pay. These bind only for employees at the very bottom of Level 1.

HRA under the 8th CPC

The precedent is clear. In 2016 the 7th CPC cut HRA from 30/20/10 to 24/16/8, reasoning that the base — the new basic pay — had grown 2.57 times, so the same percentage would have produced a windfall. It then built in an escalator: rates would rise to 27/18/9 when DA crossed 25%, and to 30/20/10 when DA crossed 50%.

Both thresholds have since been crossed — DA passed 25% in July 2021 and 50% in January 2024. Expect the same pattern again: a cut at changeover, with restoration as DA rebuilds. Because the base multiplies, the rupee amount still rises even at a reduced percentage. A Level 7 employee in a Y city drawing ₹8,082 today would draw roughly ₹20,546 at 16% of a 2.86-multiplied basic — still a substantial increase.

HRA and income tax

Under the old tax regime, Section 10(13A) exempts the least of: actual HRA received; rent paid minus 10% of salary (basic + DA); and 50% of salary in metros or 40% elsewhere. Under the new regime (the default for FY 2026-27), HRA is fully taxable — one reason employees paying high rent should compare regimes before filing (Income Tax Department).

Frequently asked questions

Which cities are X, Y and Z class?

X (population 50 lakh+): Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad. Y (5–50 lakh): about 90 cities including Lucknow, Jaipur, Patna, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Coimbatore, Indore, Nagpur. Z: everywhere else.

What if I live in government quarters?

You draw no HRA and instead pay a licence fee. If quarters are refused despite eligibility, HRA admissibility follows your department’s certificate rules.

Does HRA depend on actual rent paid?

No — for Central Government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay by city class, regardless of rent. Rent matters only for the income-tax exemption under Section 10(13A) in the old regime.

How does city classification get updated?

The Ministry of Finance reclassifies cities after each census based on population. Employees in a reclassified city get the new rate from the notified date.

What will HRA be under the 8th CPC?

Unknown. Precedent suggests rationalised percentages on the larger new basic (the 7th CPC used 24/16/8 rising to 30/20/10 as DA crossed thresholds). The rupee amount typically still increases.

Does HRA depend on the rent I actually pay?

No. For central government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay determined by your city class, regardless of what you pay in rent. Actual rent matters only for the income-tax exemption under Section 10(13A), and only in the old tax regime.

What happens to my HRA if I am allotted government quarters?

You stop drawing HRA and pay a licence fee instead. Rules on declining an allotment vary by department, but generally forfeit the allowance for that period.

Can both my spouse and I draw HRA?

Yes, if you both live in private accommodation. If you share government accommodation allotted to one of you, only that person's entitlement applies.

Is HRA taxable in the new tax regime?

Yes, fully. The Section 10(13A) exemption is available only under the old regime. Employees paying high rent should compare both regimes before filing.
