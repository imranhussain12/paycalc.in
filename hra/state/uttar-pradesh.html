HRA in Uttar Pradesh for Central Government Employees

Updated August 2026 6 notified cities · everywhere else Class Z

House Rent Allowance depends entirely on where you are posted, not where you live or own property. Uttar Pradesh has 6 cities with a notified classification; every other posting in the state falls into Class Z at 10% of basic pay.

Quick answer: Agra, Kanpur, Lucknow, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi are Class Y at 20% of basic pay. Everywhere else in Uttar Pradesh is Class Z at 10%.

Transfers within Uttar Pradesh

Your entitlement changes from the date you join a new station. A move between Agra, Kanpur, Lucknow, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi makes no difference — all are Class Y. The change that matters is between a classified city and an unclassified town: on a Level 7 basic that is ₹4,490 a month, or ₹53,880 a year, with no change to your basic pay at all.

Two cases override the rate entirely. If you are allotted government accommodation you draw no HRA and pay a licence fee instead. Where both spouses are in government service and share allotted quarters, only one entitlement applies. Leave, tour and deputation cases are covered in the HRA rules guide.

Why the rate is what it is

Classification follows census population: roughly 50 lakh and above for Class X, 5 to 50 lakh for Class Y, and everything else Class Z. The Ministry of Finance revises the list after each census, and a reclassification takes effect from the date the Department of Expenditure notifies it — employees do not need to apply.

The current 30/20/10 slab has applied since 1 January 2024, when Dearness Allowance crossed 50% and HRA was restored to its top rate. The 7th CPC had set it at 24/16/8 in 2016 with automatic restoration as DA rose. With DA now at 60%, there is no further escalator under the 7th CPC.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities in Uttar Pradesh get HRA above 10%?

6 cities have a notified classification in Uttar Pradesh: Agra (Class Y, 20%); Kanpur (Class Y, 20%); Lucknow (Class Y, 20%); Meerut (Class Y, 20%); Prayagraj (Class Y, 20%); Varanasi (Class Y, 20%). Every other posting in the state is Class Z at 10% of basic pay.

Does my HRA change if I am transferred within Uttar Pradesh?

Only if the classification changes. Moving between Agra, Kanpur, Lucknow, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi leaves it unchanged, since all are Class Y at 20%. Moving to or from a town without a notified classification does change it, because those are Class Z at 10% — worth ₹4,490 a month at a Level 7 basic of ₹44,900.

What is the HRA for a Level 7 employee in Uttar Pradesh?

₹8,980 a month in a Class Y city, against ₹4,490 in an unclassified town — a difference of ₹53,880 over a year on the same basic pay.

Is HRA in Uttar Pradesh based on the rent I actually pay?

No. For Central Government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay fixed by the classification of your posting, whatever your rent. Actual rent matters only for the Section 10(13A) income-tax exemption, and only under the old tax regime.
