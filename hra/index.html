HRA City List 2026: X, Y and Z Class Rates

Updated September 2026 30% / 20% / 10% since 1 January 2024

House Rent Allowance for Central Government employees depends on one thing: the classification of the city you are posted to. Not where you live, not what you pay in rent, and not where you own property. Three classes, three rates.

Quick answer: X class (8 metros) pays 30% of basic pay, Y class (around 90 cities) pays 20%, and Z class — everywhere else — pays 10%. Minimum HRA is ₹5,400, ₹3,600 and ₹1,800 respectively.

Z class — everywhere else

Any posting without a notified classification falls into Class Z at 10%, with a floor of ₹1,800 a month. Most district and sub-divisional postings are here, which is why field staff draw materially less than colleagues doing identical work in a state capital.

The gap widens with seniority because HRA is a percentage. At Level 13 the difference between an X city and a Z town is ₹24,620 a month on identical basic pay.

What a transfer does

Your entitlement changes from the date you join the new station. A Level 7 employee moving from an X city to a Z town loses ₹8,980 a month — ₹1,07,760 a year — with no change to basic pay at all.

Two cases override the rate entirely. If you are allotted government accommodation you draw no HRA and pay a licence fee instead. Where both spouses are in government service and share allotted quarters, only one entitlement applies.

How classification is decided

Classification follows census population: roughly 50 lakh and above for Class X, 5 to 50 lakh for Class Y, and everything else Class Z. The Ministry of Finance revises the list after each census, and a reclassification takes effect from the date the Department of Expenditure notifies it. Employees do not apply — the change flows through payroll automatically.

The current 30/20/10 slab dates from 1 January 2024. The 7th CPC had set HRA at 24/16/8 in 2016 with automatic restoration as DA rose: to 27/18/9 when DA crossed 25%, and to 30/20/10 when it crossed 50%. With DA now at 60%, this is the top slab.

Full rules on leave, tour, deputation and the tax treatment are in the HRA rules guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities are X class for HRA?

Eight: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. Each has an urban agglomeration above roughly 50 lakh. They are also the cities treated as metros for the Section 10(13A) rent exemption, where the 50% limit applies instead of 40%.

What is the HRA rate in 2026?

30% of basic pay in X class cities, 20% in Y class and 10% in Z class. These have applied since 1 January 2024, when Dearness Allowance crossed 50% and HRA was restored to its top slab. There is no further escalator under the 7th CPC.

Does HRA depend on the rent I pay?

No. For Central Government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay determined by the classification of your posting, whatever your rent. Actual rent matters only for the Section 10(13A) income-tax exemption, and only under the old tax regime — in the new regime HRA is fully taxable.

What happens to HRA if I am posted to a small town?

Anywhere without a notified classification is Class Z at 10%. On a Level 7 basic of ₹44,900 that is ₹4,490 against ₹13,470 in an X city — a difference of ₹8,980 a month, or ₹1,07,760 a year, with no change to your basic pay.

Is there a minimum HRA?

Yes: ₹5,400 in X, ₹3,600 in Y and ₹1,800 in Z per month. These floors were set at 30%, 20% and 10% of the ₹18,000 minimum pay, and in practice bind only at the bottom of Level 1.

Will HRA change under the 8th Pay Commission?

Probably. At the 7th CPC changeover HRA was cut from 30/20/10 to 24/16/8 because the new basic pay was 2.57 times larger, then restored in steps as DA rose. A similar rationalisation is likely — but because basic pay multiplies, the rupee amount would still be expected to rise.
