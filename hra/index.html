Z class — everywhere else

Any station not listed as X or Y is Z class, drawing 10% of basic pay with a floor of ₹1,800 a month. Most district and sub-divisional postings fall here, which is why field staff draw materially less HRA than colleagues doing identical work in a state capital.

How classification works

Classification follows census population and is notified by the Ministry of Finance under OM No. 2/5/2017-E.II(B). It applies uniformly across all central ministries, autonomous bodies and defence establishments. Because it is census-linked, a city can move up a class after a census, and employees there begin drawing the higher rate from the notified date.

Two practical points. Your entitlement follows your place of posting, not your home town. And if you occupy government accommodation you draw no HRA at all, paying a licence fee instead. Full rules are in the HRA rules guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities are X class for HRA?

There are eight: Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune and Ahmedabad. Employees posted in these cities draw 30% of basic pay as HRA.

What are the current HRA rates?

30% of basic pay in X-class cities, 20% in Y-class and 10% in Z-class. These rates were restored on 1 January 2024 when Dearness Allowance crossed 50%.

How is a city classified as X, Y or Z?

By census population. X cities have 50 lakh or more residents, Y cities between 5 lakh and 50 lakh, and every other place is Z class. The list is notified by the Ministry of Finance.

My city is not listed — what rate do I get?

If your posting is not in an X or Y city it is Z class, and HRA is 10% of basic pay with a floor of ₹1,800 per month. Confirm with your Pay & Accounts Office, since classification is occasionally revised.

Does HRA follow my home town or my posting?

Your place of posting. An officer posted to a Z-class town draws 10% even if their family lives in Delhi.

Verify before you act: classification is occasionally revised. Confirm your station's current class with your Pay & Accounts Office.
