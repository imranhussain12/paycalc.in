HRA in Nagpur for Central Government Employees

Updated August 2026 Nagpur, Maharashtra · Class Y city · 20% of basic pay

Nagpur is a Y-class city for House Rent Allowance, so Central Government employees posted there draw 20% of basic pay as HRA. The classification covers Nagpur and its urban agglomeration.

Quick answer: HRA in Nagpur = 20% of your basic pay, with a floor of ₹3,600 per month. On a basic pay of ₹44,900 that is ₹8,980 a month.

Why Nagpur is Class Y

City classification follows census population. X-class cities have a population of 50 lakh or more — there are only eight. Y-class cities fall between 5 lakh and 50 lakh, and everything else is Z-class. The list is notified by the Ministry of Finance and applies uniformly across ministries, autonomous bodies and defence establishments.

Your entitlement follows your place of posting, not where your family lives or where you own property. An officer posted to Nagpur draws the Y-class rate even if their family remains elsewhere, and the rate changes from the date you join a station in a different class.

HRA elsewhere in Maharashtra

Maharashtra has 5 cities with a notified HRA classification. Everywhere else in the state falls into Class Z at 10%. This matters at transfer time: moving between two towns in the same state can change your HRA substantially, even if your basic pay does not move at all.

What a transfer does to your HRA

HRA follows your place of posting, not where your family lives or where you own property. If you are transferred out of Nagpur your HRA changes from the date you join the new station, even though your basic pay is unchanged.

Two situations change this. If you are allotted government accommodation in Nagpur you draw no HRA at all and pay a licence fee instead. And where both spouses are in government service and share allotted accommodation, only one entitlement applies. The HRA rules guide covers leave, deputation and tour cases.

The rules behind the rate

Nagpur's 20% is the top slab under the 7th CPC. HRA was set at 24/16/8 in 2016 and restored in steps as Dearness Allowance rose, reaching 30/20/10 when DA crossed 50% on 1 January 2024. There is no further escalator.

For how the classification is decided, what happens in government accommodation, leave and deputation cases, and the Section 10(13A) tax exemption — where the 40% non-metro limit applies, see the HRA rules guide.

Could Nagpur change class?

Classification follows census population, and the Ministry of Finance reclassifies cities after each census. A move from Class Y to Class X requires the urban agglomeration to cross roughly 50 lakh in population. For most Y-class cities that is a long way off, but it is the reason employees in fast-growing cities watch census results closely — an upgrade would lift HRA from 20% to 30% of basic pay overnight.

When a city is reclassified, the new rate applies from the date notified by the Department of Expenditure — not from the census itself. Employees do not need to apply; the change flows through the payroll automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HRA rate in Nagpur?

Nagpur is classified as a Y-class city for House Rent Allowance, so Central Government employees posted there draw 20% of basic pay as HRA. This rate has applied since 1 January 2024, when Dearness Allowance crossed 50% and HRA was restored to the 30/20/10 slab.

How much HRA does a Level 7 employee get in Nagpur?

A Level 7 employee at the entry stage of ₹44,900 basic pay draws ₹8,980 per month as HRA in Nagpur, which is ₹1,07,760 a year.

Is HRA in Nagpur based on the rent I actually pay?

No. For Central Government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay set by your city's classification, regardless of what rent you pay. Actual rent matters only for the income-tax exemption under Section 10(13A), and only if you are on the old tax regime.

Will HRA in Nagpur change under the 8th Pay Commission?

Possibly. At the 7th CPC changeover HRA was cut from 30/20/10 to 24/16/8 because the new basic pay was 2.57 times larger, then restored in steps as DA rose. A similar rationalisation could happen under the 8th CPC — but because basic pay multiplies, the rupee amount would still be expected to rise.

Verify before you act: city classification is occasionally revised. Confirm your station's current class with your Pay & Accounts Office before relying on these figures.

Does HRA in Nagpur change if I live outside the city limits?

Your entitlement follows the station you are posted to, not your residential address. If your office is in the notified Nagpur area you draw the Class Y rate of 20%, even if you commute in from a smaller town nearby. Conversely, living in Nagpur while posted elsewhere does not entitle you to the Nagpur rate.

How much HRA would I lose if transferred out of Nagpur?

On a Level 7 basic of ₹44,900, Nagpur pays ₹8,980 a month. A Class Z posting pays ₹4,490, so the difference is ₹4,490 a month — about ₹53,880 a year.
