HRA in Pune for Central Government Employees

Updated August 2026 Pune, Maharashtra · Class X city · 30% of basic pay

Pune is an X-class city for House Rent Allowance, so Central Government employees posted there draw 30% of basic pay as HRA. The classification covers Pune Urban Agglomeration, including Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Quick answer: HRA in Pune = 30% of your basic pay, with a floor of ₹5,400 per month. On a basic pay of ₹44,900 that is ₹13,470 a month.

Why Pune is Class X

City classification follows census population. X-class cities have a population of 50 lakh or more — there are only eight. Y-class cities fall between 5 lakh and 50 lakh, and everything else is Z-class. The list is notified by the Ministry of Finance and applies uniformly across ministries, autonomous bodies and defence establishments.

Your entitlement follows your place of posting, not where your family lives or where you own property. An officer posted to Pune draws the X-class rate even if their family remains elsewhere, and the rate changes from the date you join a station in a different class.

The rate rose in January 2024

HRA is tied to Dearness Allowance. The 7th CPC set it at 24/16/8 in 2016, with automatic restoration as DA rose: to 27/18/9 when DA crossed 25%, and to 30/20/10 when DA crossed 50%. DA reached 50% on 1 January 2024, so Pune has been on 30% since then. With DA now at 60%, that is the top slab — there is no further escalator under the 7th CPC.

HRA and your income tax

Under the old tax regime, Section 10(13A) exempts the least of three amounts: the HRA actually received; rent paid minus 10% of salary; and 50% of salary in metro cities or 40% elsewhere. Pune counts as a metro for this purpose, so the 50% limit applies.

Under the new regime — the default from FY 2026-27 — HRA is fully taxable. Employees paying substantial rent are often the one group for whom the old regime still wins, so compare both in the income tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HRA rate in Pune?

Pune is classified as a X-class city for House Rent Allowance, so Central Government employees posted there draw 30% of basic pay as HRA. This rate has applied since 1 January 2024, when Dearness Allowance crossed 50% and HRA was restored to the 30/20/10 slab.

How much HRA does a Level 7 employee get in Pune?

A Level 7 employee at the entry stage of ₹44,900 basic pay draws ₹13,470 per month as HRA in Pune, which is ₹1,61,640 a year.

Is HRA in Pune based on the rent I actually pay?

No. For Central Government employees HRA is a flat percentage of basic pay set by your city's classification, regardless of what rent you pay. Actual rent matters only for the income-tax exemption under Section 10(13A), and only if you are on the old tax regime.

Will HRA in Pune change under the 8th Pay Commission?

Possibly. At the 7th CPC changeover HRA was cut from 30/20/10 to 24/16/8 because the new basic pay was 2.57 times larger, then restored in steps as DA rose. A similar rationalisation could happen under the 8th CPC — but because basic pay multiplies, the rupee amount would still be expected to rise.

Verify before you act: city classification is occasionally revised. Confirm your station's current class with your Pay & Accounts Office before relying on these figures.
